BEYOND THE REGISTERS

Look beyond the badge.

Awards, rankings and referrals are marketing claims, not official checks. Ask what sits behind them.

TOP AGENT★QUESTION THE BADGE
01 / AWARDS

Who can win?

Ask whether payment or a subscription affects eligibility.

Who runs the award, and how is it funded? Must an agent pay, subscribe or buy a package to be eligible or nominated? Are non-subscribers considered on equal terms? Are the criteria, judging process and results independently verifiable?

#1 ?QUESTION THE RANKING
02 / RANKINGS

Number one at what?

Check the area, timeframe and results behind the claim.

Number one at what, exactly? Across which dates and locations? Who supplied the data, and can I independently check the methodology?

03 / REFERRALS

Who gets paid?

Some websites receive a fee from the agent they recommend.
