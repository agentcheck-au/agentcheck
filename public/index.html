THE QUESTIONS WORTH ASKING

Look past the polish.
Find the proof.

Not every award or referral is misleading. But a polished badge cannot tell you how it was earned. Here's how to look a little closer.

TOP AGENT★QUESTION THE BADGE
01 / AWARDS

An award tells a story. Ask who wrote it.

Some programs involve paid entry, subscriptions or sponsorship. Ask whether agents who do not pay the platform can still be considered on equal terms.

Who runs the award, and how is it funded? Must an agent pay, subscribe or buy a package to be eligible or nominated? Are non-subscribers considered on equal terms? Are the criteria, judging process and results independently verifiable?

#1 ?QUESTION THE RANKING
02 / RANKINGS

“Number one” means very little without a method.

A ranking can be based on a suburb, time period, review count or selected metric. The fine print matters.

Number one at what, exactly? Across which dates and locations? Who supplied the data, and can I independently check the methodology?

FOLLOW THE MONEY
03 / REFERRALS

Follow the recommendation. Then follow the incentive.

A referral platform may introduce you to an agent, and some platforms receive a fee from that agent. Ask whether payment affects which agents are recommended.

Does the platform receive a referral fee from the agent? Are agents who do not pay or subscribe still considered? Can payment influence visibility, ranking or priority? What fees and relationships are disclosed to consumers?

Privacy & how it works

Your search is sent to AgentCheck’s server and the NSW licence service. AgentCheck does not create an account, save your searches to a database or send your reported register findings to the government. The selected public licence record and your reported result can remain in this browser tab for up to 30 minutes to help you return from the register. “Start a new search” clears this saved journey. Browser storage may remain until the tab closes; expired entries are discarded when the page next opens.

The hosting and government providers may retain operational access logs. Licence status is time-specific. AgentCheck does not independently search the Name and Shame Register, endorse an agent or assess their suitability.
