Two separate checks — both matter

1. Check the NSW property licence

Match the name, licence number and suburb. Review the status, authorised activities, dates and any conditions. A name match alone is not proof of identity.

2. Check the NSW Fair Trading Name and Shame Register

Open the official Name and Shame Register and search independently for published regulatory action. Confirm the person's name, licence number, dates and published outcome before deciding whether an entry relates to the same agent.

A current licence does not prove an absence of regulatory history. Equally, a published regulatory action does not automatically mean a licence is currently suspended or cancelled.

How we handle the information

Licence searches and detail lookups request NSW Government records when you use the service. The result shows when it was checked. Match the licence holder and review the status, conditions and dates; missing details are labelled rather than inferred.

The Name and Shame Register is a separate, manual check on the official website. Your selection on return is guidance input, not a finding verified by AgentCheck.

Our agent licence guide, agency licence guide and regulatory register guide explain each step and link to the official sources.

What AgentCheck does not do

  • We do not independently search the NSW Fair Trading Name and Shame Register or verify a visitor's selection there.
  • We do not rank, recommend, certify or endorse real estate agents.
  • We do not claim that a licence record alone establishes an agent's reputation, competence or suitability.
  • We do not provide legal advice or replace checking the official government records.

Licence information is time-sensitive and government data may be incomplete, delayed or unavailable. Always verify important details with the official source.
